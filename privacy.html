Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

CountBy5 is a study app for the Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app does not collect your personal information.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no analytics or ad SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not receive any.

Information stored on your device

Your study progress (answers, streaks, bookmarks, missed questions, flashcard status, mock exam history), your settings, and your exam date and daily goal are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Reset progress (in Settings) deletes your study progress. Your exam date and daily goal stay until you change them.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app stored on your device.

Depending on your phone's settings, your operating system may include app data in its own device backups (for example, iCloud or Google backups). Those backups are controlled by Apple or Google and your device settings, not by us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play and processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your card number or other payment details. Apple and Google handle your payment information under their own privacy policies.

Subscription processing

When in-app purchases go live, the app will check your subscription status through a subscription service, RevenueCat. To do this it uses an anonymous app user ID (not your name or email) and the purchase receipt data from the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat processes this data under its own privacy policy. This section will be confirmed, and updated if needed, when billing is enabled.

Links that leave the app

Opening the Terms of Use, this Privacy Policy, the support email, or the manage-subscription page opens your browser, email app, or the App Store or Google Play outside CountBy5. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Children

CountBy5 is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also note it in the app's release notes.
